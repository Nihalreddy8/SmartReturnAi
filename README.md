# SmartReturn AI 🧠

SmartReturn AI is an AI-powered Android app that helps people keep track of their everyday belongings and find them when they are misplaced or left behind.

We all have those moments — you leave home with your bottle, earbuds, wallet, or keys, visit a few places, and later realize something is missing. Instead of trying to remember everything yourself, SmartReturn AI is designed to act like a **digital memory for your belongings**.

## 💡 The Idea

The basic idea is simple:

**Register your belongings once → keep track of them → get help when something goes missing.**

For example, imagine you register your water bottle before leaving home. You go shopping and later return home without it.

Instead of only realizing that the bottle is missing, SmartReturn AI can help you understand:

* Which item is missing
* When it was last seen
* The last known context/location
* What places you visited
* What you can do to find it

You can also ask the AI assistant questions like:

> "Where did I last have my bottle?"

or

> "What item am I missing?"

---

## ✨ Main Features

### 📸 Scan and Register Items

Users can add their belongings by scanning or registering them in the application.

Examples:

* Water bottle
* Wallet
* Earbuds
* Keys
* Backpack
* Charger
* Books
* Other personal items

### 🗂️ My Belongings

The app maintains a list of the user's registered belongings so they can easily check what they normally carry.

### 🔍 Missing Item Detection

SmartReturn AI checks the user's belongings context and helps identify items that may be missing.

### 📍 Last-Seen Information

When an item is missing, the app can show the last available information about the item, helping the user remember where they might have left it.

### 🤖 Ask SmartReturn

The app includes an AI assistant where users can ask questions about their belongings in a natural way.

For example:

```text
User:
I can't find my water bottle.

SmartReturn AI:
Your bottle was last recorded during your
shopping trip. You can check that location
and retrace your recent activities.
```

### 🔔 Smart Alerts

The application can provide alerts when an item appears to be missing or has not been accounted for.

---

# 🧩 How It Works

The basic workflow is:

```text
Register your belongings
          ↓
      Scan / Detect
          ↓
   Build item inventory
          ↓
 Track available context
          ↓
  Detect possible missing item
          ↓
   Alert the user
          ↓
 Ask SmartReturn AI
          ↓
 Help recover the item
```
